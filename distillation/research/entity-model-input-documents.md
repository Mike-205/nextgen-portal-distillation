## Summary

**22 distinct documents** (24 source files, collapsing the two confirmed dedup pairs: 18/19 byte-identical; 12/13 two non-identical files sharing one canonical name and field set).

**Two findings flagged as significant enough to shape the entity model's shape, not just its content — surface these explicitly during synthesis rather than deciding silently:**

1. **Daily Log's shift marker vs. a real Shift entity.** The Daily Log (07) has a free-standing AM/PM/Overnight checkbox with no field linking it to an actual scheduled/clocked-in Shift record. The scope memo adds GPS time clock/attendance/payroll export as in-scope, which implies Shift will be a real, staff-scheduling-driven entity. Whether a Daily Log entry references that Shift record (and thus indirectly the staff member's clock-in) or remains a self-reported free-text marker independent of scheduling has real workflow consequences (e.g., can a Daily Log exist for a shift no one was clocked into?). This is the same *shape* of question that caused the three-round OQ-16 saga — recommend it become an explicit register entry rather than an assumption baked into the entity model.

2. **Trip Risk Assessment is a genuinely different grain — per-Excursion, many-Clients-per-document** — not per-Client or per-Site like everything else audited. It needs its own entity (an "Excursion" or "Trip" concept) with a many-to-many link to Client, distinct from every other Document type's one-Client-per-record shape. Not a client-facing open question (the form itself is internally consistent and confirmed), but a real modeling decision for item 4.

**Secondary notes, lower stakes, worth a mention but not blocking:**
- Client Incident Report's (09) "Facility Information" section may describe an external caregiver's location, not a NextGen Site — resolve before treating that section as "the Site" in the model.
- Several Program-scoped-only documents (Shift Checklist, Sharp Count, Grocery List, Behaviour Tracker, MAR, Activity Calendar) predate the Site concept entirely (glossary already notes Site "is not present as a concept in any of the 19 forms") — they'll need to move from a Program dropdown to a real Site reference in the redesign. This is expected/already implied by Site's introduction, not a new client-facing question.
- Client Intake Form's (20) "Internal Use Only" block (Eligibility Determination / Assigned Program / Assigned Case Manager / Service Start Date) reads as Placement-creation data bolted onto the intake document rather than intake data itself — worth separating into its own logical record in the entity model even if the client-facing form stays one physical document.
