## Structural Issues Found
- **Section 4 "Personal Goals" uses a generic SMART-style goal structure** (achieve / why it matters / support needed / success measure, ×3 goal slots) that is structurally unrelated to the **Healing Plan's** Medicine Wheel goal structure (Spiritual/Mental/Emotional/Physical Wellbeing, each with Program Tasks 1-3, Narrative, Indicators of Success, Goal Attainment Rating 1-5) — see Overlap section. Two entirely different goal-planning frameworks exist across these documents with no stated relationship.
- Section 8 "Safety Supports" duplicates the *category names* used in Individual Safety Plan (safety considerations, emergency considerations, community safety considerations) as three free-text boxes here, while the actual detail lives in the separate Individual Safety Plan — this is the one clean, non-duplicative cross-reference in the batch (see Cross-References), but the three free-text boxes here still invite duplicate/conflicting entry of the same information.
- Section 1's Program(s) checklist is a fourth variant of the Program/Service taxonomy problem (see Glossary Cross-Check) — it allows multi-select ("Program(s)"), which conflicts with the discovery doc's confirmed rule that "each client is assigned to one primary program at a time" (D Q7).

## Overlap With Original 19 Forms
- **Healing Plan (F04)** — both documents are person-centred goal/support plans for the same client, reviewed on a similar cadence (Quarterly appears in both), but built on incompatible frameworks (Medicine Wheel vs. generic goal template). Not clear whether ISP replaces Healing Plan, sits alongside it, or one feeds the other.
- **Individual Needs Assessment Form (F03)** — Section 5 (Daily Living Support Needs) and Section 7 (Behaviour Support) substantially duplicate F03's personal-functioning and risk-factor content, just reworded from clinical/rating-scale language (F03 uses a 6-point concern-rating scale) to a person-first narrative/checkbox format here.
- **Client Intake Form (file 20, this batch)** — Sections 2, 3, 7, 9, 11 (Client Profile, Communication, Behaviour Support, Routine, Education/Employment) cover largely the same ground as Intake Form Sections 9, 10, 12, 13, 14 — meaning the same categories of information (communication style, behavioural triggers, daily living, employment status, goals) are captured at both intake and support-planning stages, with no defined data flow between them.

## Glossary Cross-Check
- Section 1's Program(s) list (Respite Care, SIL, Community Access, Youth Services, Mental Health Support, Life Skills Support, Employment Support, Other) is a **fifth** distinct Program/Service taxonomy across the 24 forms audited so far (original 19 + these 5), matching none of the previous four exactly.
- "Case Manager/Coordinator" (Section 1) is a third phrasing for the undefined "Case Manager" role flagged in files 20 and 22.
- "Supervisor Approval" as a named signature block (vs. plain "Supervisor" in Individual Safety Plan) is a minor phrasing variant, not a substantive collision.
